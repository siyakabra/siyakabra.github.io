link
I completed the Bender Trust Research Project as part of a team, conducting an equity analysis of Energy Fuels Inc. ($UUUU) within the uranium and rare earths sector of the energy and mining industry. Together, my team used SEC filings (10-Ks and quarterly transcripts), along with company presentations and open data to build revenue forecasts, valuation models, and comparative analyses. We applied Porter’s Five Forces, SWOT analysis, potential risks and mitigants, and industry growth drivers to evaluate the company’s competitive position and long-term earnings potential. My team developed a detailed outlook on the company and assessed the appropriate valuation multiple using metrics such as forward P/E and EV/EBITDA. We concluded the project with a polished investment pitch, presenting our analysis and defending our recommendation for the stock before a panel of judges.

Project 2

Ace Tutors Program | Non-Profit Initiative

Founded and managed a youth-led non-profit providing free tutoring to K–12 students in my community. Led a team of 7 tutors to deliver 130+ instruction hours to 170+ students while expanding social media engagement to 430+ followers and 2,000+ views.

link
I started Ace Tutors during high school in the aftermath of COVID-19. As someone who lost valuable learning time because of virtual schooling, I wanted to help other students who also felt lost and struggled to transition back into in-person learning. Ace Tutors is a youth-led, non-profit online tutoring program that I founded with the mission of ensuring that students in grades K–12 receive the academic support they need in subjects such as Mathematics, English, Computer Science, and Science. Through free tutoring sessions over the summer, our team of tutors help students strengthen their existing conceptual knowledge while introducing them to new concepts that broaden their understanding. Now that all the tutors are in college, we provide Advising Sessions for high schoolers instead of standard tutoring services!
